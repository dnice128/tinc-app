# Privacy Policy for Tinc

**Effective date:** 2026-05-06

Tinc ("the app") is built and operated by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what leaves your device. The short version: **nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.**

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**

The app has no servers, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no user accounts. Everything you do in Tinc — photos you import, color cards you create, folder names, tags, titles, and saved library — stays on your device.

## 2. Permissions the App Requests

When you use certain features, iOS will ask for your permission. The app uses these permissions only for the purposes described below.

- **Photo Library (read access):** Used so you can select an existing photo to turn into a color card. Photos are read on-device only and are never uploaded.
- **Camera:** Used so you can capture a new photo to turn into a color card. Captured photos are processed on-device only.
- **Photo Library (add access):** Used only when you tap the export or save action, so the rendered color card can be written back to your Photos.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos / Camera → Tinc**.

## 3. Photo Metadata

When you import a photo, the app reads the photo's embedded EXIF data — specifically the original capture date — so the resulting color card can show when the photo was taken. This metadata is read locally and is not transmitted anywhere.

## 4. Local Storage

The app saves your color cards, folder organization, tags, and any photos you've used to create cards in the app's private storage on your device. This storage is sandboxed by iOS and is not accessible to other apps.

If you delete the app, this data is removed.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social-media services.

If and when in-app purchases are offered, those transactions are processed by **Apple** through the App Store. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive your name, payment information, or contact details from Apple — only the anonymous receipt that confirms a purchase was made.

## 6. Sharing and Exporting

When you tap the share or export action inside the app, you choose where the color card goes (Photos, Files, AirDrop, Messages, etc.). At that point your content leaves the app under your direction. The app does not perform any silent or background transmission of your content.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at the same URL where you are reading this, with a revised effective date at the top. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's App Store description.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: **tinc.app.help@gmail.com**
